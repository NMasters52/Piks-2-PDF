# Field-test walk improvements

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

Agree on behavior for recovering, editing and exporting field walks on a phone, and evaluate the feasibility and size of photo markings.

## Notes

Use Talk It Through. Count three distinct exported walks. Preserve the interrupted active walk offline. Support viewing, saving or editing recent walks. Use the Job/Lot ID as the filename. Field samples live in pdf-data.local.

## Decisions so far

- [How should reopening and editing a recent walk work?](recent-walks.md) — Preserve the active walk and offer the previous PDF for download before replacing it.

## Not yet specified

Recovery behavior if the device cannot store another walk depends on how recent walks are retained.

## Out of scope

Shipping a drawing editor during the feasibility study.
