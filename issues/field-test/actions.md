# Where should Export and New walk appear?

Label: wayfinder:prototype
Assignee: nick

Parent: [Field-test walk improvements](map.md)

## Question

What placement works during a 50-photo walk, and what confirmation protects the active walk when starting another?

## Agreed placement

Keep Export PDF and New walk visible at the top while scrolling. Put Take photo, Add photos, and Back to top in the fixed bottom toolbar on desktop and phone. The [scrollable prototype](toolbar-prototype.html) shows the chosen placement.

Place Jump to bottom beside New walk. Disable Back to top at the top of the walk.

## New walk

When the active walk has photos, New walk offers three choices.

- **Save draft** saves the active walk, including its photos and notes, then opens a fresh walk. If all three draft slots are full, choose which parked draft to replace or keep editing the active walk. Preserve the active walk until parking succeeds.
- **Discard walk** clears the active walk and opens a fresh walk.
- **Keep editing** closes the prompt and returns to the same place in the active walk.

Keep one autosaved active walk and up to three parked drafts. Only successful exports enter the separate three-walk history.

Open **Drafts** to find parked walks by Job/Lot ID, photo count, and last edit. If a walk is already in progress, opening a draft offers the same three choices. Parking the current walk uses the slot freed by the draft being opened.
