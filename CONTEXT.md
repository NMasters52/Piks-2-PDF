# Cabinet walk

A cabinet walk collects photos and optional notes for a PDF that can be attached to an existing job jacket.

## Language

**Walk**:
A collection of ordered cabinet photos and optional notes, optionally identified by a job or lot. The MVP keeps one current walk at a time.
_Avoid_: Project, job manager

**Paused walk**:
The current walk set aside for later resumption. Pausing preserves its photos, notes, and job or lot identifier.

**Export**:
A PDF containing the current walk's photos and notes. Creating an export does not clear the walk.
